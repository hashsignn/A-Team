How this board works.

  1. Choose your view in the bar above: the site you plan for, and which customers. Everything — this list, the ladder, the map — narrows to it.
  2. Pick a route. Each one ships from one site. Its colour says how soon somebody must decide, not how bad the news is.
  3. Act. The route shows its shipments and customers, key accounts first, then the options worth more than they cost. Plan recovery on a shipment opens the ways round on the map.
